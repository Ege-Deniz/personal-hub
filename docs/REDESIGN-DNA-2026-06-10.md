## Durable findings (this corpus)

1. **Two-color discipline is near-universal at the top.** Every winner above runs a duo (+ at most one accent). Validates cyan-on-abyss; demands gold stays caged.
2. **Restraint outscores spectacle on the jury sheet.** Hashgraph's near-static typographic site took the highest animation sub-score (8.6) in the set. Few, perfect, named motions beat continuous theatre.
3. **Named interactions win awards.** Every winner page credits specific elements (view switch, footer scroll transition, mouse interaction, playful footer) — not ambient effects.
4. **Numbered/coded rhythm reads as engineering.** Hashgraph's //01 sections, REF's PDM-1025 filing codes — systematic notation as aesthetic.
5. **Real product surfaces are content** (Cleo). Brain Operator's actual UI is stronger material than any abstraction of it.
6. **Motion must belong to the subject** — Pacôme (motion designer) earns motion everywhere; an AI-systems engineer earns precision, traces, and instruments instead.

## Anti-patterns (from constraint + corpus)

- Dense particle fog / soft-sprite masses (rejected by Ege; absent from every winner above).
- Per-character text flights, masked line theatrics (rejected; the corpus does clip/opacity reveals at most).
- Three-plus accents in chrome; glassmorphism as primary surface; decorative 3D unattached to content.

## Three source-backed directions (pick one)

### A — Typographic Authority (Hashgraph lane)
Type-only hero: massive static EGE DENIZ / AI-Native Developer, one ≤600ms clip
reveal on load, then still. Acts become //01–//07 numbered rhythm with hairline
rules and tabular-mono annotations. WebGL exists in exactly ONE place: a
contained, calm shard-brain inside the Brain Operator act (motion tied to
subject). Lowest noise, highest confidence register.

### B — Calm Field (quiet the existing architecture)
Keep the persistent canvas + camera acts, but: original tuned shard aesthetic
(~5k crisp tetrahedra, the warm core), no pulse wave, no scatter-boot (fade
assemble), curl at one-third, deeper dim, slower camera. All text theatrics
removed — opacity/8px reveals only, headline static. Closest to current; keeps
the through-object concept.

### C — The Instrument (REF × Cleo lane)
Hero pairs static type with a REAL artifact surface — a live trace/terminal
panel of brain-operator (actual product UI as content). Work index becomes a
filing-code ledger (BO-0426 · RW-0226 · SIBA-0925...) with one signature
interaction: hover-trace revealing real metadata per row. No ambient WebGL at
all. The most "AI engineer" statement of the three; needs real product
captures as assets.

All three keep: act/scroll architecture, Geist + Geist Mono, cyan sole accent,
abyss base, the honest catalog, Signal act, footer finale.
